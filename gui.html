ATR (Simple Moving Average)

\[ \text{TR} = \max\{ (\text{High} - \text{Low}), |\text{High} - \text{Close}_{n-1}|, |\text{Low} - \text{Close}_{n-1}| \} \] \[ \text{ATR}_n = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} \text{TR}_i \]
