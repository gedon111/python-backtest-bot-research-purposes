ATR (Simple Moving Average)

\[ \text{TR} = \max\{ (\text{High} - \text{Low}), |\text{High} - \text{Close}_{n-1}|, |\text{Low} - \text{Close}_{n-1}| \} \] \[ \text{ATR}_n = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} \text{TR}_i \]
Result: --

KDJ (Stochastic)

\[ \text{RSV} = \left[ \frac{\text{Close}_n - \text{Lowest Low}_n}{\text{Highest High}_n - \text{Lowest Low}_n} \right] \times 100 \] \[ \text{K}_n = \text{K}_{n-1} \times \frac{2}{3} + \text{RSV} \times \frac{1}{3} \] \[ \text{D}_n = \text{D}_{n-1} \times \frac{2}{3} + \text{K}_n \times \frac{1}{3} \] \[ \text{J}_n = 3 \times \text{K}_n - 2 \times \text{D}_n \]
