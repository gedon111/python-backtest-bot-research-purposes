# Results: ML meta-labeling experiment

> Generated by `python experimental/ml_meta_labeling.py --phase report` from `experimental/results/*.json`. Do not edit numbers by hand. Design: [PREREGISTRATION.md](PREREGISTRATION.md). Exploratory, and not part of the paper.

## Bottom line

- **Primary endpoint (pre-registered, one test):** A2's mean pnl per trade in 2022–2025 is **+0.200%** over **22** trades. The shuffled-label null gives +0.107% (95% of null runs fall in [-0.64, +1.07]). One-sided p = **0.378** over 200 retrains. At α = 0.05 this **does NOT reject** the null that the model's selection is no better than a model trained on noise.
- **Trade count:** A2 takes 22 trades in 2022–2025, against 27 for the locked rules on the same stream (the paper's figure is 27 on `candles.csv`).
- **Out-of-sample discrimination:** AUC 0.544. Log-loss is 0.6995 for the model vs 0.6941 for a constant 2018–21 base rate (lower is better). The OOS base rate is 49.8%.
- **Does it hold across 50 random 12-month windows (R2)?** A2's total is > 0 in 52% of windows. It beats A1 in 50%, beats A0 in 44%, and beats buy-and-hold in 16%. For comparison, A0's total is > 0 in 42% of windows.
- **Lockbox (Jan–Aug 2026):** not opened yet.

## Integrity gates

- Parity: the single-trade simulator + rule policy reproduces the locked trades exactly: **True** (27/27).
- Truncation invariance (features don't change when future bars are appended): **True** (cuts at bars 3451, 4827, 6072).
- 2022+ date guard raises inside select/freeze: **True**.
- One-bar shift test on CV log-loss (unshifted 0.6816): lagged features 0.6836 (expected: not better). Lead features, the positive control, 0.6448 (expected: better). This shows the test can detect leakage.
- Frozen model sha256 `725ed18599eae21c8f7b647f57391a37aada81e741d0c8ff77f4d4a435a354d7`, committed before the OOS run and checked by `oos`.
- Re-running `oos` with the same seeds: every non-float field is identical, and 14 float fields differ by at most 2.2e-16. So the result is not byte-identical. Cause: RandomForest(n_jobs=-1) sums per-tree probabilities in thread order; only log-loss aggregates move, at ~1e-16. All trades, counts, pnl means and p-values identical.

## Limitations

- 2018–2021 is the period the rule structure itself was formulated on (`docs/SCRATCH_RESULTS_METHODS.md`). Training there is legitimate for the ML, but the A0/B rule arms on 2018–21 are in-sample.
- A0 here starts fresh at 2022-01-01 on the extended series, with indicators warmed up since 2018. The paper's 27 come from `candles.csv`, which warms up at 2022-01-01. Here they coincide: A0 = 27 trades, +30.31%, matching the paper's locked figure.
- Candidates overlap: the same OB is touched on consecutive bars. The effective sample is closer to the number of OBs than the number of candidates, which is why CV is grouped by OB.
- One position at a time means the arms trade different subsets. Δ-mean comparisons between arms are on different trades, so they use independent rather than paired bootstraps.
- Exploratory tests (sections on indicators, R1, R2, A4) are not confirmatory, even after Holm correction.
