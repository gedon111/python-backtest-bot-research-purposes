## Limitations

- Trades are assigned to a window by entry bar. A trade still open when a window ends is counted in full in that window's trade statistics, but the paper's dollar-equity helper only books exits inside the window.
- The scaled arm scales windows only. Pattern shapes (2-bar MACD rise, the 3-bar K-acceleration and its [1, 6] range, the bar-3 KDJ exit rule, OB quality windows) stay literal, so it is only partly the same strategy in clock time.
- There are 122 missing hours in the 1h feed, mostly 2018–2021 exchange outages. They are not filled, and the engine treats bars as consecutive, the same as the 4h data.
- This is a second look at the same 2022–2025 market the paper used. A positive result here would still share that period's regime; the holdout is the only data no version of the strategy has seen.
